Express Entry Draw #435 Results: 442 Invited at CRS 760

Express Entry Draw #435: Canada Issues 442 ITAs to Provincial Nominee Candidates

Draw Date: August 17, 2026  |  Draw Type: Provincial Nominee Program (PNP)  |  Invitations Issued: 442  |  CRS Cutoff: 760

Draw Summary: What Happened

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) conducted Express Entry Draw #435 on August 17, 2026, issuing 442 Invitations to Apply (ITAs) exclusively to candidates who hold a valid provincial nomination. The minimum Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score required to receive an invitation was 760 points.

This was a Provincial Nominee Program-specific draw, one of the most common draw types IRCC runs alongside general and category-based rounds. Under this format, all invitations are reserved for Express Entry candidates who have already secured a nomination from a Canadian province or territory through one of the participating Provincial Nominee Programs. No candidates without a valid nomination were eligible to receive an ITA in this round, regardless of how high their base human capital score may be.

What the 760 CRS Cutoff Actually Means

A cutoff of 760 in a PNP-specific draw can appear alarming at first glance—and understandably so. However, it is critical to understand that this number is not a reflection of raw human capital alone.

When a candidate receives a provincial nomination through the Express Entry-linked stream of any PNP, IRCC automatically awards them an additional 600 CRS points on top of their existing profile score. This 600-point boost is by far the single largest factor in a PNP draw's cutoff score.

What this means in practice: the majority of candidates who received an ITA in Draw #435 likely had a pre-nomination CRS score in the range of approximately 150 to 160 points before the provincial nomination was applied. In other words, if you hold a provincial nomination and your base CRS score is anywhere near that range, you are a strong candidate for an invitation in future PNP-specific rounds.

Candidates in the Express Entry pool without a provincial nomination should not use this cutoff as a benchmark for their own competitiveness. Your pathway and the draws relevant to you are fundamentally different.

Trend Context: How Draw #435 Compares to Recent PNP Rounds

PNP-specific draws have remained a consistent feature of IRCC's Express Entry draw schedule, typically occurring every two to four weeks alongside general and category-based rounds. The volume of invitations in recent PNP draws has fluctuated based on the size of the nominated candidate pool, provincial nomination allocations, and IRCC's broader annual immigration targets under the Federal Skilled Worker, Canadian Experience Class, and Federal Skilled Trades programs.

An invitation volume of 442 ITAs in this round reflects a moderate-sized draw, consistent with IRCC managing inventory carefully as it balances PNP rounds with other draw categories. The cutoff of 760 points is likewise within the expected range for this draw type, where scores consistently land between 720 and 800 due to the standardized 600-point nomination bonus.

Candidates who have been waiting in the pool with a provincial nomination should take note: if your total CRS score including the nomination bonus is at or above the 760 threshold, your profile is well-positioned for an upcoming invitation.

Who Benefits Directly from This Draw

Draw #435 delivers direct and immediate value to a specific group of candidates:

  • Express Entry candidates with an active provincial nomination from a participating PNP stream who had a total CRS score of 760 or higher as of August 17, 2026.
  • Candidates who applied through PNP Express Entry-aligned streams such as Ontario's Human Capital Priorities stream, British Columbia's Skills Immigration, Alberta's Express Entry Stream, or equivalent programs in other provinces.
  • Candidates whose base human capital score was approximately 150 points or higher, since adding the 600-point nomination bonus would bring their total to or above the 760 cutoff.

For these individuals, receiving an ITA means they can now submit a complete application for Canadian permanent residence within the 60-day window provided by IRCC.

What Applicants Just Below the Cutoff Should Do Now

If you hold a provincial nomination and your total CRS score—including the 600-point bonus—fell just below 760 in this draw, do not be discouraged. PNP-specific draws recur regularly, and the cutoff fluctuates with each round depending on the size and composition of the eligible pool.

Here is what you should do immediately:

  1. Verify your profile is complete and up to date. Ensure your Express Entry profile accurately reflects your current job offer, language scores, education credentials, and work experience. Any outdated information can artificially suppress your score.
  2. Confirm your provincial nomination is still valid. Provincial nominations have expiry conditions. Confirm with your province that your nomination remains active and has been properly linked to your Express Entry profile.
  3. Improve your language scores if possible. Even modest gains on an IELTS or CELPIP retake can add meaningful points to your base human capital score, pushing your total above future cutoff thresholds.
  4. Monitor IRCC draw announcements closely. PNP draws are held frequently. Staying informed about each round helps you anticipate when your score may be sufficient for an invitation.
