Express Entry Draw #454 Results: 1999 Invited at CRS 673

Express Entry Draw #454: Canada Invites 1,999 Provincial Nominees — November 23, 2026

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) conducted Express Entry Draw #454 on November 23, 2026, issuing 1,999 Invitations to Apply (ITAs) exclusively to candidates holding a valid provincial nomination. The minimum Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score required to receive an invitation was 673 — a figure that, at first glance, may seem extraordinarily high, but requires important context to understand correctly.

Draw Summary: What Happened on November 23, 2026

This was a Provincial Nominee Program (PNP)-specific draw, one of several distinct draw types that IRCC conducts within the Express Entry system. Unlike all-program draws or category-based draws — which are open to candidates across the Federal Skilled Worker Program, Federal Skilled Trades Program, and Canadian Experience Class — this round targeted only those candidates who had already secured a provincial or territorial nomination.

Nearly 2,000 candidates received ITAs, giving them the opportunity to submit a complete permanent residence application to IRCC. These individuals had previously gone through a separate provincial or territorial immigration stream, been selected by a province or territory, and subsequently had their CRS score boosted by the standard 600-point provincial nomination bonus applied automatically upon nomination confirmation in their Express Entry profile.

What the 673 Cutoff Actually Means for Applicants

The 673 CRS cutoff can be misleading if read without context. Because every candidate in this draw held a valid provincial nomination, each of them received an automatic addition of 600 CRS points to their base score. This means the actual human capital score — based on factors such as age, education, language ability, and Canadian work experience — needed to reach the cutoff was approximately 73 points.

A base score of 73 is well within reach for the vast majority of Express Entry candidates. In practical terms, almost any candidate who secures a provincial nomination will comfortably clear the threshold in a PNP-specific draw. This draw should therefore cause no concern whatsoever for candidates sitting in the general pool without a nomination, as the 673 figure bears no relevance to what will be required in upcoming all-program or category-based rounds.

How Draw #454 Compares to Recent PNP Draws

PNP-specific draws have consistently produced CRS cutoffs in the 660 to 730 range, almost entirely as a reflection of the 600-point nomination bonus rather than exceptionally strong base profiles. The 673 cutoff in Draw #454 falls comfortably within that historical norm, suggesting the composition of the provincial nominee pool has remained stable and that IRCC continues to process PNP-stream candidates at a steady pace.

The invitation volume of 1,999 is also consistent with recent PNP draw sizes, which have typically ranged between 1,000 and 2,500 invitations per round. IRCC has maintained regular PNP-specific draws as part of its broader strategy to fulfil provincial and territorial labour market needs while distributing immigration across regions beyond major urban centres.

Who Specifically Benefits From This Draw

Draw #454 directly benefits candidates who meet all of the following criteria:

  • They hold an active Express Entry profile in one of the three eligible federal programs (FSWP, FSTP, or CEC).
  • They have received and confirmed a valid nomination from a Canadian province or territory through an Express Entry-aligned provincial stream.
  • Their updated CRS score — including the 600-point nomination bonus — met or exceeded 673 as of the draw date.

Provinces and territories that regularly nominate candidates through Express Entry-linked streams include Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Nova Scotia, and Prince Edward Island, among others. Candidates nominated through any of these streams and sitting in the pool on November 23, 2026 would have been eligible for consideration in this draw.

What Applicants Just Below the Cutoff Should Do Now

If your profile was in the pool on draw day and you did not receive an ITA, the first step is to verify your current CRS score and ensure your profile information is complete and up to date. Even a small discrepancy in how your credentials, language scores, or employment history are recorded can cost you critical points.

Beyond profile accuracy, candidates who do not yet hold a provincial nomination should actively explore PNP options. Securing a nomination is by far the most reliable path to boosting your CRS score into a competitive range. Research the streams offered by provinces aligned with your occupation, work experience, and intended destination in Canada.

If you are already nominated but did not receive an ITA this round, do not be discouraged. IRCC conducts PNP draws regularly, and with your 600-point bonus in place, your chances of receiving an invitation in a future round remain very strong. Continue monitoring your application and ensure your profile remains valid and active.

Finally, candidates who are not yet nominated should focus on maximising their base CRS score through additional language testing, completing further education, or accumulating more Canadian work experience — all of which can meaningfully improve your standing ahead of category-based or all-program draws.
